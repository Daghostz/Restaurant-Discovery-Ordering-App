# Project Charter

## Project Information
* **Project Name:** Restaurant Discovery & Ordering App
* **Group Name:** NutriDine Team
* **Target Stage:** MVP (Minimum Viable Product) Lab 2-3

## Project Overview
The Restaurant Discovery & Ordering App is a personalized dining platform designed to help users decide what and where to eat based on their physical location, budget constraints, user ratings, and nutritional goals. By integrating transparent calorie counts into the browsing and ordering process, the application empowers users to make informed, health-conscious dining decisions seamlessly.

## The Problem
Diners often struggle to balance multiple factors when deciding where to eat: distance, budget, food quality, and dietary tracking. Checking restaurant ratings, finding menus with accurate prices, and calculating nutritional intake usually requires switching between several different apps or search engines. This fragmented process makes everyday meal planning and food ordering stressful, overwhelming, and time-consuming.

## Target Users
* **Health-conscious individuals** who want to track their daily calorie intake effortlessly while dining out.
* **Budget-conscious students and professionals** looking to filter dining options strictly by specific price tiers ($, $$, $$$).
* **Busy users** who need a quick, reliable way to find nearby food, check the menu, and place an order without friction.
* **Food enthusiasts** looking to explore highly-rated local restaurants in their immediate vicinity.

## Main Features (MVP Scope)
* **Geolocation-Based Discovery:** Automatically look up and display a feed of restaurants situated directly around the user's current location.
* **Rating & Price Filtering:** Empower users to narrow down their dining choices by filtering out low-rated venues or restaurants outside their budget.
* **Calorie-Aware Menus:** Select any restaurant to view its full menu, with explicit total calorie counts prominently displayed next to the price and description of every food item.
* **Seamless Food Ordering:** Select items to build a virtual cart, view a comprehensive summary (calculating both total order price and total order calories), and finalize the checkout.

## Future Development (Out of Scope for MVP)
* **AI-Powered Recommendations:** Smart suggestions based on a user's past orders and favorite flavor profiles.
* **Advanced Dietary Filters:** Specific allergen and lifestyle tags for menu items (e.g., vegan, gluten-free, keto, halal).
* **Live Delivery Tracking:** Real-time GPS mapping to track the driver from the restaurant to the user's door.
* **Smart Shopping / Loyalty:** Reward points for healthy eating and recurring orders.
